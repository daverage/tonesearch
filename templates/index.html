About TONE Search

What is a NAM capture?

Neural Amp Modeler is a free, open-source way to capture a real amp, pedal or full rig as a small model file (.nam). Load it in a NAM player and your guitar goes through a faithful copy of that gear.

Where do the captures come from?

Every pack is a real upload on TONE3000, the community library of NAM captures. TONE Search does not host or change them; downloads come straight from TONE3000 and credit their creators.

How good are the matches?

The fit score is the AI's judgement of how close a pack gets to your tone, based on its gear, tags and description. It is a starting point, not a verdict: try the top few and trust your ears.

Tips for better results

Name the amp if you know it. Say whether you want a head (to pair with your own cab) or a full rig. Refine in plain words, such as "more gain", "darker" or "cheaper amp". Turn on web research for songs and artists so the gear comes from real sources.

AI can be wrong. Tone briefs, gear lists, tips and fit scores are generated by AI and may be inaccurate. They are suggestions, not facts: trust your ears.

Settings

Use your own keys instead of this site's.

Your keys stay private. They're saved only in this browser. Each search sends them securely (HTTPS) to this site's server, which uses them for that one request to contact TONE3000 and your AI provider, then forgets them. They are never stored, logged or shared, and never included in exports.

Please note: this page shows Google ads. Like any script on a web page, the ad code can technically read what's saved in your browser for this site, including these keys. Use keys you can revoke, ideally with a spending limit, and remove them with Clear all when you're done.

No hourly limit with your own keys: add both an AI key and a TONE3000 key. With only one of them, searches still count toward this site's hourly limit.
